Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my purchase?

Immediately after completing checkout via Whop or Gumroad, you will receive an instant download link on screen as well as a receipt email containing direct access to your files.

Can I buy through Whop or Gumroad?

Yes! Every product card offers direct links to both Whop and Gumroad. You can choose whichever platform you prefer for checkout.

How do I edit or customize these slots for my own store?

Use the "Store Config" button in the top header to enter your global Whop or Gumroad username/URLs. You can also click "Add Product" to test adding custom items directly on the page!
